# Sales Dashboard

Interactive sales analytics dashboard visualising $382K in 
sales data across regions, categories, and time.

## Overview

This project analyses sales transactions across 5 global 
regions and 6 product categories, providing insights into 
revenue trends, customer ratings, and regional performance.

## Data Summary

- **Total Revenue:** $382,027
- **Regions:** Asia Pacific, Europe, Latin America, 
  Middle East Africa, North America
- **Categories:** Beauty, Books, Clothing, Electronics, 
  Home & Garden, Sports
- **Time Period:** January – December

## Key Insights

- Latin America leads in regional sales at $81,980
- Beauty is the top product category at $69,920
- January recorded the highest monthly sales at $50,455
- Average customer rating: 3.01 / 5

## File

- `Sales_Data.xlsx` — raw data with pivot tables for 
  monthly trends, regional sales, category breakdown, 
  and customer ratings
